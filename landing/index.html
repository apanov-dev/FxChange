Built in Cyprus.
Designed for smarter exchange.

FxChange is being built by a Computer Science student in Cyprus, after noticing how fragmented currency exchange can be. Different exchange desks, fintech services and P2P offers can provide different rates, making comparison unnecessarily difficult — FxChange aims to fix that.
